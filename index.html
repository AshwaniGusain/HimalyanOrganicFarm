About Us

Rooted in Himalayan Communities, Built for Global Supply Chains

Himalyan Organic Farm is a B2B sourcing organization focused on millet, with the ability to expand into other organic products. We work with farming communities in the mountain regions of Uttarakhand to support quality sourcing, traceability, and documentation suitable for international buyers.

  • Origin focus: Sourcing from Himalayan high-altitude farming areas in Uttarakhand, India.
  • Organic focus: Sourcing from farms that follow organic, residue-free production practices, with documentation prepared per buyer requirement.
  • Farmer engagement: Building partnerships with farmer households and grower groups across multiple Himalayan villages.
  • Quality discipline: Documented post-harvest handling, batch coding, and third-party lab testing arranged per shipment on request.
  • Community focus: Working to channel agricultural advisory support and access to government schemes to partner farming communities.
  • Social commitment: Long-term aim to support education and women-led initiatives in partner farming villages as supply volumes grow.

Export readiness: Shipments are prepared with documentation, packaging, and lab-test options to match typical buyer requirements in European, UK, US, and Australian markets. Specific documentation scope is agreed per lot and per buyer.

Bulk Organic Millets for B2B Buyers

Our product range covers organic millet grains and milled blends sourced from Himalayan farming communities. Lab testing, documentation, MOQ, and packaging options are agreed per buyer requirement and per shipment.

Finger Millet (Ragi)

Calcium-rich Himalayan grain, suitable for nutraceutical, cereal, bakery, and health-food brand applications.

Typical profile (indicative):
• Protein: around 6.8–7.5%
• Fiber: around 3.5–4.2%
• Calcium: generally high vs. common cereals
• Moisture: target <12% at dispatch

Documentation options:
• Organic, residue-free production focus
• Destination-market documentation prepared on request
• Per-lot third-party lab testing available

Packaging: 25kg, 50kg, 1MT jute bags (custom options on request)
Indicative MOQ: 500kg (subject to confirmation)

Values are indicative and vary by crop year, variety, and growing region. Actual specifications are confirmed per lot.

Barnyard Millet

Light, nutrient-rich grain from high-altitude Himalayan fields, suited to premium cereal, breakfast, and wellness product formulations.

Typical profile (indicative):
• Protein: around 6.2–6.8%

• Low glycemic index profile vs. polished cereals
• Moisture: target <11% at dispatch

Documentation options:
• Organic, residue-free production focus
• Destination-market documentation prepared on request
• Per-lot residue and heavy-metal testing available

Packaging: 25kg, 50kg, 1MT jute bags (custom options on request)
