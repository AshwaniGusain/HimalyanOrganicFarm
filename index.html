About Us

Rooted in Himalayan Communities, Built for Global Supply Chains

Himalyan Organic Farm is a certified B2B sourcing organization focused on millet and expandable to other organic products. Our network supports quality sourcing from mountain regions of Uttarakhand with full traceability and compliance.

  • Government Registration: Registered with Government of India (Ministry of Agriculture) and Government of Uttarakhand (Department of Agriculture).
  • Organic Certification: NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production) certified. Compliant with EU Organic Certification (EC 834/2007).
  • Farmer Network: Tied up with 10,000+ Himalayan farmer households across Uttarakhand's high-altitude regions.
  • Quality Assurance: ISO 9001:2015 compliant processes. Third-party lab testing for every batch. Residue-free certification available.
  • Community Impact: Supports farmers through zero-interest credit, government scheme access, and agricultural advisory services.
  • Social Responsibility: Funds child education programs and women empowerment initiatives in partner farming communities.

Export Standards Compliance: All products meet stringent requirements for European, UK, US, and Australian markets including pesticide residue limits, heavy metal testing, and allergen protocols.
