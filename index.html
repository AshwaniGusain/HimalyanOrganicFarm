About Us

Rooted in Himalayan Communities, Built for Global Supply Chains

Himalyan Organic Farm is a B2B sourcing organization focused on millet and expandable to other organic products. Our network supports quality sourcing from mountain regions of Uttarakhand.

  • Registered with Government of India and Government of Uttarakhand.
  • Tied up with 10,000+ Himalayan farmer households for millet collection.
  • Supports farmers through zero-interest credit, scheme access, and advisory.
  • Promotes child education and women empowerment in partner communities.
